# Bernoulli Density

> [!info] Supporting Material
> - [[Intro to ML - Ethem Alpaydin]]

> [!summary] Summary
> A Bernoulli density models one trial with two outcomes: the event occurs, or it does not. It is the two-class case used with maximum likelihood estimation. The multinomial density is the same idea for more than two mutually exclusive outcomes.

> [!important] Key Points
> - There are two outcomes.
> - The distribution parameter is estimated by maximum likelihood.
> - More than two classes uses the multinomial density instead.

## Notes

- Two outcomes: the event occurs or it does not.
- [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]] for the distribution.
- The generalization to more than two classes is [[Multinomial Density]].

# Multinomial Density

> [!info] Supporting Material
> - [[Intro to ML - Ethem Alpaydin]]

> [!summary] Summary
> A multinomial density is the several-outcome version of a Bernoulli density. Each trial lands in one of a set of mutually exclusive states, rather than in one of two states.

> [!important] Key Points
> - It generalizes the Bernoulli density.
> - The outcomes are mutually exclusive.
> - It is the discrete parametric model for more than two classes.

## Notes

- Generalized version of [[Burnoulli Density|Bernoulli density]]. Instead of two states, the outcome is one of many mutually exclusive states.

# Mean Square Error

> [!summary] Summary
> Mean square error is a way to judge an estimator, including one produced by maximum likelihood. These notes introduce it as that quality measure and do not yet record the bias-variance decomposition.

> [!important] Key Points
> - It evaluates estimator quality.
> - [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]] is the estimator these notes connect it to.
> - The decomposition into variance and squared bias is recorded under [[Parametric Methods]].

## Notes

- Evaluate the quality of an estimator from something like [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]].

## Related Concepts

- [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]] - the estimator being judged.
- [[Parametric Methods]] - states $\mathrm{MSE} = \mathrm{Var}(d) + \mathrm{bias}(d)^2$.

# CANDID-PTX

> [!info] Supporting Material
> - [[Foundation X.pdf]]

> [!summary] Summary
> In these notes, CANDID-PTX is a chest X-ray dataset that supplies labels and boxes for classification, segmentation, and localization. The only source recorded so far is a citation in Foundation X. The dataset's size, label definitions, and split are not written down here.

> [!important] Key Points
> - Used as a dataset that has all three Foundation X supervision types.
> - The note does not yet separate image-level labels from boxes and masks.
> - Source: Foundation X, page 1.

## Notes

- Has labels and boxes for [[classification]], [[segmentation]], and [[localization]]. [[Foundation X.pdf#page=1&annotation=1225R|Foundation X, p.1]]

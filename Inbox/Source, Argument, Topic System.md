# Source, Argument, Topic System

> [!summary] Summary
> This is a three-layer note practice. Source notes hold what a document says. Topic notes interpret claims. Argument notes hold ideas and questions that cut across those sources and topics.

> [!important] Key Points
> - Source notes stay close to the document.
> - Topic notes interpret claims rather than copying them.
> - Argument notes are for ideas or questions.
> - A claim can move from a source note, through a topic note, into an argument.

## Layers

1. Source notes.
2. Topic notes: interpretation of claims.
3. Argument notes: ideas or questions.
